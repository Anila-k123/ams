# Demo script: Kannan vs Seetharaman (O.S. No. 900/2025)

This demo follows one real matter through a law firm, **from the first walk-in to the client seeing his invoice**. Nothing is set up in advance: no client, no case, no tasks, no drafts. Each person signs in and does what they'd do in a real office, in the order it would happen.

Each step says:
- **Situation:** what's happening in the office.
- **Why:** the reason for doing it this way.
- **How:** what to click and type.
- **What to point out:** what the system does for them, and what that person can't see.

Password for every staff login: `Demo@1234`. Base URL: `http://localhost:5173`. Allow about 35 minutes.

## Before the meeting

1. Start everything with `run-project.bat`. **The court scraper on port 8000 must be up**, because step 2 imports the case from eCourts.
2. **Reset to a clean start.** From `Advocate-app-BE-Django`:
   ```bat
   venv\Scripts\python.exe manage.py reset_demo_client --name Kannan --firm rajesh@kumar-associates.demo
   venv\Scripts\python.exe manage.py reset_demo_client --name Kannan --firm rajesh@kumar-associates.demo --yes
   ```
   The first command lists what would be deleted. The second deletes Kannan, his case and everything created for them, with a backup to `uploads\demo-resets\`. If it says "No client named 'Kannan'", you're already clean. **Run it after every rehearsal.**
3. Have the two files in `docs/demo-files` ready:
   - **`Court_fee_receipt_-_OS_900-2025.pdf`**: the paper Kannan hands over (step 2).
   - **`Mediation brief - precedent.docx`**: the firm's brief from an earlier matter, which Priya drafts from (step 5).
4. Hard-reload the browser (Ctrl+F5). Use a private window per login, or sign out between roles. In Lisa, clear old chats (**Clear History**).

## Step 1: Kannan walks in (Lakshmi, Receptionist)

**Situation:** Kannan arrives at the front desk with a folder of court papers. He isn't a client yet.

**Why:** before anyone works on a matter, the firm records **who the client is and how to reach him**. Every later step hangs off this record: the case, the bills, the notices, his portal login. Lakshmi also notes **which advocate will take the matter**, so it doesn't sit unassigned. The front desk decides who to route it to; it doesn't do the legal work.

**How:**

| Screen | Input |
|---|---|
| Sidebar **Clients** → **Add New Client** | **Name** `Kannan` · **Description** `Landlord; suit for possession and arrears against tenant` · **Email** `kannan@clients.demo` · **Phone** `+91 90030 11900` · **Billing Currency** INR · **Handling Advocate** **Rajesh Kumar** |
| Client's Address (same form) | **Building** `No. 7` · **Street** `Kamarajar Salai` · **City** `Mylapore` · **District** `Chennai` · **State** `Tamil Nadu` · **Pincode** `600004` · **Country** `India` → save |

**What to point out:**
- Rajesh is **told at once**, in the app and by email if he has email switched on. Nobody has to walk over and tell him.
- Lakshmi's pick list only offers people who can take a case, so a matter can't be handed to another receptionist.
- The full address is saved in parts. Later the invoice uses it without anyone retyping it (step 7).
- **What Lakshmi can't see:** the sidebar has no Documents, Tasks, Invoices, Drafting or legal reference. The front desk handles people and appointments, not case papers or money.

## Step 2: Rajesh takes over the matter (Senior Advocate)

### 2a. He's notified, and brings the case in from the court

**Situation:** Rajesh sees the notice: a new client, Kannan, assigned to him. He needs the case in the system before he can do anything with it.

**Why import instead of typing it in:** the court already holds the official record: the parties, both sides' lawyers, every hearing date, interim applications, the judge, and the current stage. Typing it by hand is slow and error-prone. Importing takes the court's own record and **keeps it linked** so it can be refreshed later.

**How:**

| Screen | Input |
|---|---|
| Bell icon in the header (**Notifications**) | **"New client assigned to you - Kannan"**, with his phone, email and address, and "Added by: Lakshmi S." → **Got it** |
| Sidebar **Cases** → **Workspace** → add a case | Opens **Add Case to Workspace** → **Available Courts** → **District Courts** |
| District cascade | **State** `TAMIL NADU` → **District** `Chennai` → **Court Complex** `City Civil Court Complex, Chennai` |
| Search tab **Case Number** | **Case Type** `OS - Original Suit` · **Case Number** `900` · **Case Year** `2025` → **Search For Case** |
| Result | "Fetched from … — review and save". Check it's **Kannan Vs Seetharaman** → **Assign to client** `Kannan` → **Save Case to Workspace** |

**What to point out:** the case, the parties and their lawyers came in from the court with no typing. So did the **next date**: **13 Oct 2026, Mediation** is already under Hearings, and the whole team will be reminded before it.

### 2b. He studies the file

**Situation:** Rajesh is new to this matter. Before advising Kannan, he needs to know what happened in court over the last 18 months.

**Why:** a senior can't take a mediation position without knowing the history: what was filed, what the court decided, where it stands now. Normally that means reading a thick file. Here the court record is on screen, and Lisa can summarise it in seconds.

**How:**

| Screen | What he looks at |
|---|---|
| Case Detail → **Parties** | Kannan (plaintiff; previous counsel M. Venugopal) against Seetharaman |
| **Extra Details** | The interim applications. IA/1/2025 and IA/2/2025 are both **disposed**, so nothing is pending on the side. The case moved from the IV to the XVIII Assistant Judge |
| Court record (**Extra Details** / **Timeline**) | The listing history, 21 sittings: evidence, then trial, now mediation. **Hearings** holds only what's coming up: 13 Oct, Mediation |
| **Acts** | CPC Order VII, the rules for filing a suit |
| **Actions** → **Refresh court data** | Checks with the court that nothing has changed since the import |
| Sidebar **Lisa** | "Ask Lisa anything..." → `summarise the Kannan case`. Then ask `when is its mediation and what is pending before that?` |

**What Lisa should say:**
- **Summary:** an Original Suit filed 21 Jan 2025 and registered 13 Feb 2025. It went through evidence and trial, moved to the XVIII Assistant Judge, and is now in mediation.
- **Where it stands:** mediation on 13 Oct 2026.
- **Second question:** she stays on this case without being told which one, and answers the mediation date.

**What to point out:** Lisa answers from the court record and the firm's own data only, and says so if she doesn't know. She remembers which case "its" means.

### 2c. He records what he's concluded

**Situation:** having read the file and spoken to Kannan, Rajesh knows the key fact for strategy: **Kannan will settle if he gets his property back**.

**Why a note:** advocates keep a **case diary**, a running record of decisions and instructions. If Rajesh is in court when Priya works on the brief, or someone else picks the matter up, the note tells them the client's position without a phone call. The tag flags the matter on the case list.

**How:**

| Screen | Input |
|---|---|
| **Notes** tab | "Write a case note (diary entry)..." → `Took over from previous counsel. Referred to mediation on 13 Oct. Client instructs: open to settlement if possession is restored; wants arrears but will accept instalments.` → **Add Note** |
| **+ tag** | **Follow Up** |

### 2d. He files the client's papers

**Situation:** Kannan brought his papers, including the **court fee receipt**: proof he paid the court fee when the suit was filed.

**Why upload it:** the firm keeps a copy of every paper the client hands over, **on the case**, so anyone on the matter can find it without asking for the physical folder. This receipt matters twice: it's proof of filing, and it's the basis for recording the court-fee expense in step 7. It will also be **shared back to Kannan** in his portal (step 6), so he has it too.

**How:**

| Screen | Input |
|---|---|
| **Documents** tab | **Choose file** → `docs/demo-files/Court_fee_receipt_-_OS_900-2025.pdf` → **Upload** → **See Summary** |

**What to point out:** the system reads the document and writes a short **AI summary**, so nobody has to open every file to know what it is.

### 2e. He plans the work and delegates it

**Situation:** mediation is on 13 Oct. Before that, Rajesh needs a written **mediation brief** (the firm's settlement position) and some research on what Kannan is entitled to.

**Why delegate:** a senior's time goes on strategy and court. Drafting goes to the junior and research to the intern, **with deadlines that leave time for his review** before the 11 Oct client meeting. Assigning in the system means each person sees their task, the deadline is tracked, and the finished work comes back to him for approval.

**How** (**Tasks** tab, inline form):

| Task | Priority | Deadline | Assign to |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Prepare mediation brief for client meeting` | Medium | 9 Oct 2026 | Priya Nair → **Add** |
| `Research: recovery of possession, arrears of rent and mesne profits` | Medium | 10 Oct 2026 | Karthik R. → **Add** |

**What to point out:** each person is notified. The tasks show under Tasks for the whole team, with who assigned them and when they're due.

### 2f. He keeps watch on the listing

**How:** sidebar **Cases** → **Daily Causelist** / **Display Board**.

**Why:** courts publish each day's list of cases. The firm sees when O.S. 900/2025 is listed without anyone checking the court website every morning.

## Step 4: The research (Karthik, Intern)

**Situation:** Karthik has Rajesh's research task. Rajesh wants to know what Kannan can claim beyond the rent itself.

**Why:** a mediation position is only as strong as the law behind it. If Kannan is entitled to **mesne profits** (money for the period the tenant stayed on unlawfully), that's leverage in the negotiation.

**How:**

| Screen | Input |
|---|---|
| Sidebar **Tasks** → **Assigned to me** | `Research: recovery of possession…`, from Rajesh |
| Sidebar **Acts** | `Code of Civil Procedure` → **Order VII** |
| Sidebar **Legal Dictionary** | `mesne profits`, `possession`, `mediation` |
| Sidebar **Law Codes** | The BNS lookup |
| Tasks → the task → **Submit work** | **What was done?** `Mesne profits run from the date of suit until possession is delivered. Mediated settlements commonly allow arrears in instalments.` · **Hours spent** `1.5` → **Submit for review** |

**What to point out:**
- The research goes **back to Rajesh for review**, marked **Awaiting review**, with the hours recorded.
- **What Karthik can't do:** upload or delete documents, or see any money.

## Step 5: The mediation brief (Priya, Junior Advocate)

**Situation:** Priya has Rajesh's task: a mediation brief, due 9 Oct.

**Why start from a precedent:** lawyers don't write a brief from a blank page. They take the firm's brief from an earlier, similar matter and adapt it. The drafting engine works the same way: it takes the **structure and wording of a precedent** and rewrites it with this case's facts. The precedent here is a mediation brief from an earlier landlord–tenant suit (A. Sundaram vs P. Mohan, O.S. No. 412/2023). The draft stays **linked to the task**, so when it's done it goes straight to Rajesh for review, not by email.

**Important:** the engine copies the structure of the **first** reference document. Pick the precedent, not the court fee receipt, or you'll get a rewritten receipt.

**How:**

| Screen | Input |
|---|---|
| Case Detail (O.S. 900/2025) → **Documents** tab | **Choose file** → `docs/demo-files/Mediation brief - precedent.docx` → **Upload**. The firm's past work is now available to draft from |
| Sidebar **Tasks** → **Assigned to me** | `Prepare mediation brief for client meeting` → **Draft for this task** (pencil icon) |
| **How would you like to begin?** | **Upload reference files** → **Next** |
| **Documents** | The case is already linked. **Add Documents** → pick **Mediation brief - precedent** only. The first time it's used, it's prepared for drafting |
| **Template** | **Skip — no template** |
| **Facts & Prompt** | Paste the facts below into **Prompt / case facts** |
| **Review** | **Generate Draft** (about 40 seconds) |
| Editor | The same four sections as the precedent (**Background, Reliefs claimed, Settlement options, Points to confirm with the client**), now about Kannan. **Check nothing from the old matter is left**: no "Sundaram", "Mohan", "412/2023", "18 March 2024" or "Rs. 4,32,000". Use **Edit** for any fix → **Save** |
| **Lisa · drafting assistant** | `Tighten the settlement options into short numbered points.` → **Accept** |
| **Submit to task** | The brief is filed on the case and goes to Rajesh as **Awaiting review** |

Facts to paste into **Prompt / case facts**:

> Case: O.S. No. 900/2025, Kannan vs Seetharaman, City Civil Court, Chennai. The Court referred the parties to mediation; the sitting is on 13 October 2026.
> Plaintiff: Kannan, owner of the suit premises at No. 15, Luz Church Road, Mylapore, Chennai 600004. Defendant: Seetharaman, the tenant, under a lease dated 1 April 2021 at a monthly rent of Rs. 15,000.
> The defendant stopped paying rent from January 2025 and has not vacated.
> Reliefs claimed: recovery of possession, arrears of rent from January 2025, mesne profits from the date of suit until possession is delivered, and costs.
> Settlement terms: possession restored within 90 days; arrears paid in three equal monthly instalments; security deposit of Rs. 45,000; each side bears its own costs.

**What to point out:** the brief took under a minute and follows the firm's own format. Everything specific to this case came from the facts; everything else is the firm's tested wording.

**Also:** the firm needs a **Vakalathnama**, the authority letter that lets Rajesh appear for Kannan now that he's taken over. It's a standard form, so it comes from a template:

| Screen | Input |
|---|---|
| Sidebar **Drafting** → **Templates** → **Vakalathnama** → **Use template** | **Document title** `Vakalathnama - O.S. No. 900/2025` · **Parties:** Kannan (Plaintiff), Rajesh Kumar (Advocate) · **Purpose** `Authority for Rajesh Kumar to appear for the plaintiff in O.S. No. 900/2025, City Civil Court, Chennai.` → **Generate Draft** |

**What Priya can't see:** Invoices, Expenses, Reports, or any money on her dashboard. Typing `/dashboard/invoices` sends her back.

## Step 6: Rajesh reviews the work, and opens Kannan's portal

**Situation:** both pieces of delegated work are waiting for him.

**Why a review step:** the senior is responsible for what goes to the client and to the mediator. Nothing a junior writes is final until he approves it. If it needs work, he **sends it back with a note**, and the note stays with the task, so there's a record of what was asked and done.

**How:**

| Screen | Input |
|---|---|
| Sidebar **Tasks** → **To review** | Karthik's research and Priya's brief |
| Karthik's research → **Approve** | The research is accepted; the task completes |
| Priya's brief → **Open draft** | **Read-only**, with the banner "Reviewing Priya Nair's draft … awaiting your review" |
| **Request changes** | "What should Priya change?" → `State the arrears as a figure: 21 months (Jan 2025 to Sep 2026) at Rs. 15,000 = Rs. 3,15,000. Keep the mesne profits fallback.` → **Send back** |
| *(sign in as Priya)* | The task shows **Changes requested** with Rajesh's note. **Open draft** → **Edit** → in *Reliefs claimed*, write the arrears as `arrears of rent of Rs. 3,15,000 for January 2025 to September 2026`. Check the *Settlement options* still end with the mesne profits fallback, which Karthik's research supports → **Save** → **Submit to task** (version 2) |
| *(back to Rajesh)* | **Open draft** shows version 2 → **Approve**. The task completes and the draft locks |
| **Download** → **Word on PactPro letterhead** | The final brief on the firm's letterhead, ready to take to mediation |

**Then he gives Kannan online access.**

**Why:** clients ask "what's happening with my case?" constantly. A portal lets Kannan see his dates, bills and papers himself, and only the papers the firm **chooses** to share with him.

|---|---|
| Sidebar **Documents** → court fee receipt → users icon ("Share with the client in the portal") | Now **Shared**. The **brief stays unshared**: it's the firm's internal negotiating position |
| Sidebar **Clients** → Kannan → **Logins** | **Email** `kannan@clients.demo` · **Name** `Kannan` → **Create & send link** → **Copy** the one-time link (valid 72 hours). Keep it for step 8 |

## Step 7: Billing the work (Suresh, Accountant)

**Situation:** the firm has taken over the matter and prepared for mediation. It's time to bill the professional fee. Kannan has paid part of it upfront, and the court fee he paid needs recording.

**Why each record:**
- **Invoice:** a proper **GST tax invoice** is legally required for professional fees.
- **Payment:** the money received against the case, so the outstanding balance is always known.
- **Expense:** costs on the matter, like the court fee, so the firm knows what the case costs and what to recover.

**How:**

| Screen | Input |
|---|---|
| Sidebar **Invoices** → **Generate Invoice** | **Associated Case** O.S. 900/2025 · **Invoice Date** today · **Due Date** 30 days out |
| **Particulars** | `Professional fee: taking over the matter and mediation, O.S. 900/2025` · **₹ Amount** `25000` |
| **Recipient / GST details** | The **Billing address** and **State** fill in by themselves from Lakshmi's client record in step 1. No GSTIN: Kannan is an individual. **GST treatment** **Forward charge (firm charges GST)** · **GST rate (%)** `18` → **Raise Invoice** |
| Sidebar **Expenses** → O.S. 900/2025 row → **Payment** | **Add Client Payment:** **Amount** `10000` · **Payment Mode** UPI · **Reference / Transaction No.** `UPI-900-001` · **Description** `Advance against the mediation fee` → **Save** |
| Same row → **Add** | **Add Expense:** **Title** `Court fee stamps` · **Amount** `4850` · **Select Category** Court Fees · date · **Description** `Court fee paid at filing (receipt on file)` → **Save** |
| Same row → **View** | **Case Financial Overview:** billed, received and spent |
| Sidebar **Reports** | Revenue, outstanding amounts, expenses |

**What to point out:**
- The address came from the front desk's entry. Nobody retyped it.

- **What Suresh can't do:** cases and clients are view-only for him. He has no Documents, Drafting, Hearings or Tasks. Accounts sees the money, not the legal file.

## If something goes wrong

- **Search shows 503, or no result:** the scraper on port 8000 is down. Check the values: TAMIL NADU / Chennai / City Civil Court Complex, Chennai / OS - Original Suit / 900 / 2025.
- **"Client already exists" or "Case number already exists":** the last rehearsal wasn't reset. Run both reset commands and start again from step 1.
- **No bell notice for Rajesh:** Handling Advocate was left empty in step 1. Reset and start again from step 1.
- **No "Draft for this task" pencil:** the task is completed or cancelled, or the login has no drafting permission.
- **Generate Draft is slow or fails:** the drafting model is busy or unreachable. Wait a minute and retry once.
- **Open draft shows 404:** the person signed in didn't assign the task and has no TASK_ASSIGN. Use Rajesh.
- **Afterwards:** run the reset again, so the next run starts clean.
