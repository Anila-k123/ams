## Step 1: Advocate (Priya) registers the walk-in client

**Story:** R. Murugan walks in. He lost the original suit and wants to appeal.

| Screen | Input |
|---|---|
| Clients → **Add New Client** | Name `R. Murugan`, email `r.murugan@clients.demo`, phone `+91 94440 00700`. **Handling Advocate: Arjun Menon**. Address: Building `No. 3`, Street `Gandhi Street`, City `Tambaram`, District `Chengalpattu`, State `Tamil Nadu`, Pincode `600045`, Country `India`. Save |
| (Sign in as Arjun) | The bell shows **"New client assigned to you - R. Murugan"**, with the client's contact details and who added him. The Clients list shows **Advocate: Arjun Menon** |

**Point out:**
- Anyone who can add a client picks the advocate who will take the matter. Only people who can open a case are offered: Rajesh, Arjun, Meena and Priya, never the accountant.
- The case details go to Arjun outside the app for now (by phone, or on paper). The notice says so, and in step 2 he opens the case himself.
- Email follows each advocate's own setting. It's **off** for all demo logins, so only the in-app notice arrives. To show the mail too, turn on Arjun's email notifications under Settings first. It still won't be delivered, because his address ends in `.demo`.
- There's no front desk: any advocate can register a client.

| Screen | Input |
|---|---|
| Cases → Add case → court search | Court: **High Court → Madras High Court**. Bench: **Principal Bench of Madras High Court** (Chennai). Case type: **`AS(FIRST APPEAL)-1`**. Number `700`, year `2025`. Search, then Import. The result shows CNR `HCMA011266642024` |

| Case Detail → Hearings | Check the next hearing date that came in from the court. Click **Refresh from court** to show a live pull |

| Case Detail → Tasks → New task | Task 1 to **Priya**: `Draft grounds of appeal and memorandum`, due in 3 days, priority High. Task 2 to **Karthik**: `Research: precedents on misappreciation of evidence in first appeals (Sec. 96 CPC)`, due in 2 days |

**Pick the right case type.** The dropdown has several "first appeal" entries, and only `AS(FIRST APPEAL)-1` finds this case:

| Option | Code | Use it? |
|---|---|---|
| `AS(FIRST APPEAL)-1` | 1 | **Yes.** A.S. 700/2025 is filed under this code |
| `AS B(FIRST APPEAL BENCH)-105` | 105 | No: first appeals heard by a Division Bench |
| `AS S(FIRST APPEAL SINGLE)-106` | 106 | No: the single-judge variant |
| `AS(MD)(FIRST APPEAL(MD))-244` | 244 | No: Madurai Bench only |
| `TR AS(TRANSFER FIRST APPEAL)-128` | 128 | No: transferred first appeals |

**Point out:** the whole case record comes from the court with no typing. The Display Board and Daily Cause List (menu) show when A.S. 700/2025 is listed, and cause-list alerts go to the team.

## If something goes wrong

- The court search or Refresh from court shows 503: the scraper on port 8000 is down. Restart it, or use the existing case record.
- Import says the case already exists: the old copy (id 99) wasn't deleted. Use the existing record.
- A menu item is missing for a role: check the permissions in Role Management. They are the seeded defaults from `seed_role_demo`.
